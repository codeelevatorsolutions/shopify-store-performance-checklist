# Shopify SEO Checklist: How to Improve Your Store's Visibility on Google

Want more potential customers to discover your Shopify store through Google?

A well-optimized Shopify store needs more than attractive product pages. Search engines must be able to discover and index important URLs, understand your products, and recognize that your content answers shoppers' questions.

This practical Shopify SEO checklist covers technical SEO, keyword research, product optimization, site speed, internal linking, content marketing, and performance tracking.

## 1. Set Up Google Search Console

Google Search Console helps you understand how your store appears in Google Search.

Checklist:

- [ ] Verify ownership of your website.
- [ ] Submit your Shopify sitemap at `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Review the Page indexing report for important pages that aren't indexed.
- [ ] Inspect priority product, collection, and blog URLs.
- [ ] Monitor clicks, impressions, average position, and search queries.
- [ ] Investigate unexpected indexing changes or technical errors.

Shopify generates a sitemap automatically. You still need to confirm that your important pages are discoverable and that Google can process the sitemap.

Official guide: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo/find-site-map

## 2. Research and Map Keywords

Keyword research helps you understand how shoppers search for your products.

Examples of keyword types:

- **Product keywords:** specific product names and attributes.
- **Collection keywords:** product categories and styles.
- **Commercial keywords:** best products, comparisons, and buying advice.
- **Informational keywords:** product guides, how-to articles, and frequently asked questions.

Checklist:

- [ ] Identify relevant keywords using search data and keyword research tools.
- [ ] Understand the search intent behind each target query.
- [ ] Assign a primary topic to each important page.
- [ ] Use related phrases naturally in page copy.
- [ ] Avoid creating multiple pages that target the same intent without a clear reason.
- [ ] Prioritize relevant keywords that can attract potential buyers.

Do not choose keywords only because they have high search volume. Relevance to your products and customers matters.

## 3. Optimize Product and Collection Pages

Product and collection pages are essential parts of an ecommerce SEO strategy.

Checklist:

- [ ] Write a unique, descriptive title for each important product.
- [ ] Create useful product descriptions instead of copying manufacturer text.
- [ ] Add accurate product specifications, benefits, sizes, and materials where relevant.
- [ ] Write clear SEO titles and meta descriptions.
- [ ] Use descriptive headings that explain the page content.
- [ ] Add useful image alt text.
- [ ] Use descriptive, consistent URLs.
- [ ] Link to relevant products and collections.
- [ ] Keep product prices and availability accurate.
- [ ] Review product structured data and eligible rich-result features.

Prioritize helping shoppers make informed decisions. Avoid keyword stuffing and repetitive descriptions.

## 4. Improve Technical SEO

Technical problems can prevent search engines from accessing or understanding important pages.

Checklist:

- [ ] Check for broken internal links and unnecessary redirect chains.
- [ ] Confirm important pages return the correct HTTP status.
- [ ] Review canonical URLs and investigate unexpected duplicates.
- [ ] Check that important products and collections aren't accidentally blocked.
- [ ] Make sure navigation links are crawlable.
- [ ] Review indexing issues in Google Search Console.
- [ ] Test structured data where appropriate.
- [ ] Check that HTTPS works correctly.
- [ ] Review the website after theme changes, migrations, or major app installations.

Shopify handles several SEO foundations automatically, including sitemap generation and canonical tags. Custom themes, apps, and code changes can still introduce issues, so verify the actual implementation.

Google's ecommerce SEO guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce

## 5. Improve Site Speed and Mobile Experience

A store should be easy to use on mobile devices and perform smoothly during browsing and checkout.

Checklist:

- [ ] Test key pages with Google PageSpeed Insights.
- [ ] Review Core Web Vitals and address significant problems.
- [ ] Compress oversized images and use appropriate image formats.
- [ ] Remove unused apps and unnecessary scripts.
- [ ] Review heavy animations and third-party integrations.
- [ ] Test navigation, product variants, forms, and cart functionality on mobile.
- [ ] Check the mobile layout for overlapping text and difficult-to-use buttons.

Test important product and collection pages individually. A good homepage score does not guarantee that every template performs well.

PageSpeed Insights: https://pagespeed.web.dev/

## 6. Strengthen Internal Linking

Internal links help shoppers navigate your store and help search engines discover relationships between pages.

Checklist:

- [ ] Link important collections from your main navigation.
- [ ] Link related products where it genuinely helps shoppers.
- [ ] Connect relevant blog articles to products and collections.
- [ ] Use descriptive anchor text.
- [ ] Make important pages accessible through the website's navigation.
- [ ] Review links pointing to redirected or removed pages.

For example, a buying guide about running shoes could link to relevant running-shoe collections and products.

## 7. Publish Helpful Content

Useful content can attract visitors who are researching a product before purchasing.

Ideas include:

- Product comparison guides
- Buying guides
- Frequently asked questions
- Product care instructions
- Tutorials and how-to articles
- Detailed category guides
- Answers to common customer questions

Checklist:

- [ ] Choose topics that address real customer needs.
- [ ] Provide original insights and accurate information.
- [ ] Structure articles with clear headings.
- [ ] Add relevant internal links.
- [ ] Update outdated information.
- [ ] Avoid publishing thin articles solely to target keywords.

## 8. Earn Relevant Backlinks

Backlinks from relevant, trustworthy websites can help people discover your store and contribute to your site's authority.

Checklist:

- [ ] Build relationships with relevant industry publishers.
- [ ] Offer original research, useful resources, or expert commentary.
- [ ] Seek legitimate product reviews and relevant brand mentions.
- [ ] Contribute genuinely useful guest articles where appropriate.
- [ ] Recover relevant unlinked brand mentions when possible.
- [ ] Monitor backlinks and investigate suspicious patterns.

Avoid buying large quantities of links, automated link schemes, or mass-submitting your website to low-quality directories. Focus on relevance and editorial value rather than backlink counts alone.

## 9. Track Results and Fix Issues

SEO requires measurement and ongoing improvements.

Review these metrics regularly:

- Google Search Console clicks and impressions
- Search queries and average position
- Indexing status of important URLs
- Organic landing-page traffic
- Product and collection page engagement
- Organic conversions and revenue, where analytics is configured
- Backlink quality and relevant referral traffic

Prioritize fixes based on business impact. Resolving a serious indexing issue on a key collection may be more valuable than making a minor change to a page that already performs well.

Remember: no checklist can guarantee a particular Google ranking. Results depend on competition, relevance, technical quality, and many other factors.

## 10. Monthly Shopify SEO Checklist

Use this checklist as part of your regular store maintenance.

- [ ] Review Search Console performance.
- [ ] Inspect indexing errors and important excluded pages.
- [ ] Check broken links and redirects.
- [ ] Audit priority product and collection metadata.
- [ ] Test important pages for mobile usability and performance.
- [ ] Publish or improve useful content.
- [ ] Review internal links and relevant backlink opportunities.
- [ ] Check whether organic visits and conversions are improving.
- [ ] Revisit your priorities based on actual data.

## Final Thoughts

A strong Shopify SEO strategy combines useful product information, clear site structure, technical reliability, relevant content, and regular performance monitoring.

Start with your most important pages, fix issues that prevent discovery or usability, and measure the results before moving to the next task.
